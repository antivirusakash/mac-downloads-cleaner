How it decides

Age only decides what gets looked at: a download untouched for 60+ days. What it is decides the confidence. Moving to a new Mac or restoring a backup wipes open history, so "no open recorded" means unknown, and age alone never makes anything safe.

safe a byte-identical copy exists somewhere that stays: elsewhere in Downloads, in ~/Documents, iCloud Drive, or another drive (each copy is compared byte for byte, and files inside zips by checksum). Also zips already unzipped next to themselves, and installers for apps in Applications. A safe item never relies on a copy that another group would delete.

likely things you can get again: AI images, stock photos and music, Figma kits and fonts, Figma frame exports, screenshots, app builds and data exports, ebooks, other people's résumés, and zips that are 90%+ copied elsewhere (the reason names the few files only found here). Kinds come from file names, types, and page counts.

Purpose signals look at whose a file is and whether its job is over: a candidate's résumé after hiring, a travel quote after the trip, a vendor's price list, an invite after the event, a web page printed to PDF. They read the PDF's text and the app that made it (a browser, Google Docs, Canva, a travel agent's software). Anything that mentions your name, handle or brands never moves on these signals, and a trip or event dated in the future stays in review.

review things that may be the only copy of your own work or memories: your creatives and decks, camera photos, recordings, and anything no rule recognised.

keep ID, tax, bank and legal papers, found by name (bank, statement, tax, agreement, visa...) or by Spotlight finding ID or tax terms for your country inside the text. These are listed so you can see the space, and never ticked by default.

"Last used" is the newest of when the file arrived, changed, or was opened in an app. "Saves" counts disk space actually freed: copies that share storage with another file count as zero. Folders that hold a git repo or an app or photo library are never flagged.
